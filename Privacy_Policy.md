# MinesWeeper Privacy Policy

**Effective date: December 1, 2026**

This policy explains what information the MinesWeeper apps for Android and iOS ("the app") collect, why, and how you can delete it. MinesWeeper is a free app built and run by Miguel Saavedra Lozano ("I", "me").

## What the app collects

| Information | Where it comes from | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email address** | The email you sign up with, or the one your Google or Apple account shares | To create and sign in to your account |
| **Name and profile photo** | Your Google account, if you sign in with Google. Apple shares your name only if you allow it, and the app doesn't show it | To show your photo in the game |
| **Account ID** | Created by Firebase Authentication when you sign up | To link your game records to your account |
| **Game records** | Each game you win: number of mines, board size, time taken and date | To show your best times and keep them in sync across your devices |
| **Crash reports and diagnostics** (Android only) | Firebase Crashlytics, when the app crashes: device model, Android version, app version and the error | To find and fix bugs |
| **Usage analytics** (Android only) | Firebase Analytics: anonymous events such as app opens and screens viewed | To understand how the app is used and improve it |

If you sign in with Apple and choose **Hide My Email**, I receive only Apple's private relay address, not your real email.

The app does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or payment information, show ads, sell your information, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Where your information is stored

Your account and game records are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database). A copy of your game records and your mine-count setting is also kept on your device so the app works offline. Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Google processes this information on my behalf as a service provider, under its own terms and privacy policy:

- [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Sign in with Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), if you use it

I don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## How long information is kept

- **Your account, email, name and game records** are kept until you delete your account.
- **Crash reports** are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
- **Analytics data** is kept by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months, and isn't linked to your name or email.
- **The copy on your device** is deleted when you log out, delete your account or uninstall the app.

## Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all of your game records at any time:

- **In the app:** open the account menu on the game screen, choose **Settings**, then **Delete account**. You'll be asked to sign in again to confirm.
- **Without the app:** follow the steps on the [account deletion page](delete_account.html).

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, email, name and all your game records from Firebase. This can't be undone.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact me and I'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
