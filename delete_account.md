# Delete your MinesWeeper account

You can delete your MinesWeeper account, and all the information stored with it, at any time. This applies to both the Android and iOS apps.

## Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

1. Open MinesWeeper and sign in.
2. On the game screen, tap your profile picture (top right) to open the account menu.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
5. Sign in again when asked (with Google, Apple, or your password). This confirms it's really you.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Option 2: Ask me to delete it (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, send an email to **msmikeescom@gmail.com** with:

- the subject line **Delete my MinesWeeper account**, and
- the email address you used to sign in.

Please send it **from that same email address** so I can confirm the account is yours. I'll delete the account within **30 days** and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and sign-in details
- Your email address, name and profile photo link
- All of your game records (times, mine counts and board sizes)

## What may be kept

- **Crash reports** (Android only) are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. They contain device and error details, not your name or email.
- **Anonymous usage analytics** (Android only) are kept by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months. They aren't linked to your name or email.

Deleting your account can't be undone. If you sign up again later, you'll start with no records.

See the [privacy policy](Privacy_Policy.html) for more about what the app collects.
